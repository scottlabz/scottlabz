Analytics Reporting Platform Case Study

Transforming Complex Data Into Trusted Business Intelligence

Bringing retail, gameday, and online data into a single reporting platform built to hold up as a trusted source of business intelligence.

Project Overview

Project Analytics Reporting Platform
Organization Hawkeye Fan Shop, The Official Retailer of the University of Iowa Athletics Department
Focus Reporting Platform Architecture, Data Consolidation, Business Intelligence
Outcome A centralized platform giving the business one trusted source of truth across every sales channel

The Challenge

As reporting needs grew beyond individual dashboards, the business needed something more durable: a platform that could pull retail, gameday, and online data together into one place, hold up under the volume of a full sports calendar, and remain trustworthy as new stores, sports, and seasons were added.

Key challenges included:

  • Complex, multi-channel data that needed to be consolidated without losing detail
  • Reporting volume that scales sharply around gameday, roster changes, and postseason runs
  • A growing footprint of stores, mobile gameday shops, and online sales that all needed to feed the same platform
  • Stakeholders across the athletics department, retail operations, and the online business relying on the same platform for different purposes
  • A need for a platform that stays reliable as the partnership continues to expand

The Approach

The platform was built around consolidation and durability - pulling complex data together in one place while keeping it structured enough to remain trustworthy as volume grew.

Platform Architecture

Designed a reporting platform structured to pull retail, gameday, and online data together without losing the detail each source provides.

Data Consolidation

Brought data from every sales channel into one consistent structure, so numbers could be compared across stores, sports, and seasons.

Scalable Reporting

Built the platform to absorb sharp gameday volume spikes without slowing down or losing accuracy.

Business Intelligence Enablement

Structured the platform so stakeholders could pull trusted answers directly, rather than requesting a custom report each time.

The Solution

A centralized reporting platform now gives the business one trusted source of truth, turning complex, multi-channel data into business intelligence stakeholders can rely on.

Capability Impact
Data Consolidation Retail, gameday, and online data brought into one consistent structure.
Platform Reliability Reporting that holds up under sharp gameday volume spikes.
Trusted Intelligence A single source of truth stakeholders can query directly with confidence.

The Results

With a centralized reporting platform in place, store and web performance have improved, gameday traffic has been handled without the site buckling under peak demand, and conversion has trended upward across departments over the course of the partnership.

  • A single trusted reporting platform spanning every sales channel
  • Gameday traffic handled reliably at peak demand
  • Upward conversion trends across departments
  • A reporting platform supporting ongoing email campaigns for gameday information, product drops, and student-athlete appearances

Technologies & Capabilities

Reporting Platform Architecture

A centralized structure designed to consolidate retail, gameday, and online data without losing detail.

Data Consolidation

Multi-channel data brought together into one consistent, comparable structure.

Scalable Infrastructure

A platform built to absorb gameday volume spikes and keep pace as the partnership grows.

Business Intelligence

Structured reporting that gives stakeholders trusted answers they can query directly.

Business Impact

Complex, multi-channel data only becomes useful once it lives somewhere stakeholders can trust it. A centralized reporting platform meant retail, gameday, and online performance could be compared side by side, giving the business one dependable answer instead of three conflicting ones.

Through consolidated platform architecture, scalable reporting infrastructure, and business intelligence built for direct use, this initiative created a trusted reporting foundation that has supported the partnership with Hawkeye Fan Shop and the University of Iowa Athletics Department since 2016.
