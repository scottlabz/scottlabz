The Black & Gold Shop Case Study

Running a Reliable Storefront Through Measurement, Engineering, and Infrastructure

A combined look at the measurement, application development, and infrastructure work behind The Black & Gold Shop - the official retailer of the University of Iowa Hawkeyes.

Project Overview

Project Digital Storefront Partnership
Organization The Black & Gold Shop, The Official Retailer of The University of Iowa Hawkeyes
Focus Measurement Strategy, Application Development, Digital Experience, Infrastructure
Outcome A dependable storefront with accurate tracking, a tuned experience, and infrastructure built to handle gameday demand

The Challenge

The Black & Gold Shop is a separate business from other Iowa Hawkeyes retail partners, but it faces the same underlying pressure: demand that moves at the speed of college sports rather than steady, predictable traffic. A gameday, a roster announcement, or a postseason run can send visitors and orders far past a typical day within minutes, and the storefront has to hold up without warning.

Key challenges included:

  • Traffic and order volume that spikes sharply around live sporting events rather than growing gradually
  • A need for measurement and reporting that stakeholders could trust without a dedicated analytics background
  • A storefront experience that had to stay fast and intuitive even when thousands of fans arrived at once
  • Infrastructure and security requirements that scale with gameday demand, not average-day traffic

What Was Built

Rather than treating measurement, experience, and infrastructure as separate projects, the work was approached as one connected system - each discipline reinforcing the others.

Discipline What It Delivered
Measurement Strategy A clear plan connecting business goals to the specific events and metrics worth tracking on a gameday storefront.
Analytics Implementation Structured event tracking and a consistent data layer, validated before launch rather than patched after the fact.
Reporting & Dashboards Views built around what stakeholders actually check - sell-through, campaign performance, and gameday activity.
Digital Experience Behavioral analysis and structured testing used to smooth out friction in browsing, cart, and checkout.
Performance & Infrastructure Hosting, caching, and deployment practices built to absorb sharp traffic surges without slowing down.
Security Edge protection and secure checkout handling to keep fan data safe during high-traffic windows.

The Results

With measurement, experience, and infrastructure working together, The Black & Gold Shop has been able to handle gameday-level demand reliably, give stakeholders reporting they trust, and keep improving the storefront based on real behavior rather than guesswork.

  • A storefront that holds up under sharp, gameday-driven traffic spikes
  • Reliable measurement and reporting stakeholders can act on with confidence
  • An experience continually refined using behavioral data and structured testing
  • Infrastructure and security built to scale as demand grows

Business Impact

A retail storefront tied to a live sports calendar doesn't get the luxury of a slow ramp-up. Measurement, experience, and infrastructure all had to work together from day one, so The Black & Gold Shop could trust its data, keep the storefront fast under pressure, and give Hawkeye fans a shopping experience that matches the energy of gameday itself.

Through structured measurement, deliberate application development, and infrastructure built for real-world demand, this initiative has given The Black & Gold Shop a foundation it's built on since 2008.
