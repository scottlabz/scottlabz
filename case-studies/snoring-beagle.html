Snoring Beagle Case Study

A Dog Bed Store Built Around How Dogs Sleep

Scott Labz built the Snoring Beagle store from scratch: dog beds, crate pads and covers made in the USA, matched to the way each dog sleeps and shipped straight from the maker, with no warehouse and no inventory.

Project Overview

Project A custom online store for a dog bed brand
Organization Snoring Beagle, a dog bed brand with the tagline "Beds for dogs who take sleep seriously"
Timeline Built in October 2026. Live October 20, 2026
At launch 14 beds, crate pads and covers, with 262 size and color options
Services Storefront, product catalog, bed finder, checkout, gift cards, shipping and sales tax, order emails, email list, search data, accessibility, security, and launch checks
Live site snoringbeagle.com

The Challenge

Snoring Beagle needed to sell another maker's beds under its own brand, without buying stock or running a warehouse. Each order is placed with the maker at wholesale and shipped straight to the customer. The client asked for a store on its own code, with full control, rather than a store on a hosted platform.

That model raised questions a standard store doesn't answer on its own:

  • Prices, sizes, colors, stock and photos all live with the maker, and change without notice
  • Shipping is billed by the maker after each order, so the store has to charge enough up front without overcharging
  • Every bed comes in four to six sizes and up to six colors, and shoppers rarely know which size or shape suits their dog
  • The brand sells only on its own site, and needed to say so plainly

A custom build answered each one, on code the client owns, without add-on apps or monthly platform fees.

A catalog that keeps up with the maker

The full collection, 14 beds, crate pads and covers, comes from the maker's own product data. One command refreshes every price, size, color, sold-out size and photo, so the store matches the maker without anyone retyping it.

  • The store's own names and copy for each product sit on top of the maker's data, so a refresh never overwrites them

Straight to checkout

Checkout opens a hosted payment page in the brand's colors.

  • The Doggie Bag opens as a drawer without leaving the page, and its own page suggests what goes well with the bed and the gift card
  • A hosted checkout in the brand's colors takes cards, digital wallets and a pay-later option. Card details only ever reach the payment page
  • Prices are set on the server from the catalog, never taken from the browser, and a sold-out size is refused at checkout
  • Sales tax is added automatically from the shipping address

Shipping priced from the carriers' own rates

The maker bills shipping after each order, so the store needed a price it could charge up front. The shipping table is built from the carriers' published rates: every size, by its weight, to every shipping zone from the maker's warehouse.

  • Shipping is free on orders of $250 or more in the lower 48, and the table checks that every bed's free shipping fits inside its margin
  • Each product's search data includes its shipping cost and delivery time

The Results

Snoring Beagle went live on October 20, 2026, with checkout, gift cards, shipping, sales tax and order emails in place from day one.

  • All 38 pages checked on phone and desktop, with no errors, broken images or sideways scrolling
  • All 262 size and color options sent through checkout: every one in stock charged the right price and shipping, and every sold-out one was refused
  • Every link, form, filter and the bed finder's 24 matches checked before launch
  • A page-quality audit of the live homepage scored 100 for performance on desktop, and 100 for accessibility and SEO
  • The product search data passed a structured data validator with no errors or warnings
  • The order emails scored 10 out of 10 in an inbox deliverability test

Business Impact

A small brand selling another maker's products usually ends up on a hosted platform, paying monthly for apps that each solve part of the problem. Snoring Beagle didn't have to: the catalog refresh, the bed finder, the shipping table and the order follow-through all shipped as part of one store the client owns.

With no inventory to buy and every order placed with the maker only after it's paid for, the store's costs follow its sales. Through custom store development and privacy-safe measurement, Snoring Beagle starts with a store that answers the sizing question before the cart, prices shipping so no order loses money, and can add a new brand with one entry in its data.
