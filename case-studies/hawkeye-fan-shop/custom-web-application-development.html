Custom Storefront & Infrastructure Case Study

Building Scalable Digital Solutions Through Modern Application Architecture

Standing up a full-scale ecommerce storefront from the ground up - domain, hosting, and application - and keeping its inventory in sync with retail stores across the state.

Project Overview

Project Custom Storefront & Infrastructure
Organization Hawkeye Fan Shop, The Official Retailer of the University of Iowa Athletics Department
Focus Ecommerce Application Development, Domain & Hosting Setup, Retail Integration, Inventory Synchronization
Outcome A full-scale storefront built from scratch, with stock counts that match reality across every store, warehouse, and the website

The Challenge

This engagement was a true ground-up build, won through a competitive bid to take over from a previous provider. There was no existing site, domain configuration, or hosting to inherit - every piece needed to be established before a single product could go live. Once live, the storefront had to answer a harder, ongoing question: how do you keep inventory accurate across retail locations statewide, mobile gameday shops, and a growing online store, all while demand spikes hard around gameday, roster changes, and postseason runs?

Key challenges included:

  • No existing domain, hosting, or web application to build from
  • A need for a storefront with the full range of capabilities fans expect from their team's official shop
  • Retail locations across the state and mobile gameday shops that all needed to reflect the same stock counts as the website
  • A real risk of overselling product that wasn't actually available once demand spiked
  • A launch timeline tied to a live sports season, with no flexibility to slip
  • Hosting and deployment infrastructure that needed to support a fast-moving, gameday-driven release schedule

The Approach

The build started from the ground floor and worked outward - domain and hosting first, then the application itself - and treated every store, shop, and the website as one connected system rather than separate operations that happened to sell the same products.

Foundation Setup

Established domain registration, DNS, and hosting as the starting point for everything built on top of it.

Ecommerce Application Development

Built a full-scale storefront application with the browsing, cart, and checkout capabilities fans expect from an official team shop.

Retail Integration

Connected the online application to retail store and gameday shop operations so the business could run as one system, not several disconnected pieces.

Inventory Synchronization

Connected online inventory with retail and gameday shop stock counts so numbers matched reality across every location.

Hosting & Deployment Strategy

Built hosting and deployment practices that supported a fast-moving release schedule tied to a live sports calendar.

Component-Based Architecture

Structured the application in reusable, maintainable pieces designed to expand as the partnership added more sports and seasons.

The Solution

A custom-built ecommerce application now runs the storefront end to end, tied directly into retail operations and inventory across the state.

Capability Impact
Full-Scale Storefront A ground-up ecommerce application with every capability fans expect from their team shop.
Connected Retail Operations Online and in-store business running in unison rather than as separate systems.
Connected Inventory Stock counts matching reality across every store, warehouse, and the website.
Reliable Hosting & Deployment Infrastructure that supports a fast-moving, gameday-driven release schedule.
Scalable Architecture An application built to expand cleanly as the partnership grew.

The Results

With a custom-built application running the storefront and inventory connected across every location, store and web performance have improved, gameday traffic has been handled without the site buckling under peak demand, and conversion has trended upward across departments over the course of the partnership.

  • A full-scale storefront built and launched from the ground up
  • Inventory synchronized across stores, gameday shops, and the website

Business Impact

Building an ecommerce application from nothing means every decision - hosting, architecture, integration - shapes how well the business can run years later. A storefront can also only sell what it actually has: connecting inventory across every store, gameday shop, and the website meant fans saw accurate stock no matter where they shopped, and the business stopped overselling product that wasn't really there.

Through foundational setup, custom application development, and inventory synchronization, this initiative created a storefront that has supported the partnership with Hawkeye Fan Shop and the University of Iowa Athletics Department since 2016.
