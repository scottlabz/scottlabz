Website Performance & Security Case Study

Strengthening Digital Platforms Through Security & Performance

Keeping a gameday storefront fast, protected, and available under sharp traffic spikes, not just on a quiet Tuesday afternoon.

Project Overview

Project Website Performance & Security
Organization Hawkeye Fan Shop, The Official Retailer of the University of Iowa Athletics Department
Focus Load Speed, Caching, Asset Delivery, Edge Security, Payment Handling, Uptime Monitoring
Outcome A storefront that stays fast, protected, and available through every gameday surge

The Challenge

A retail site is judged by how it holds up under pressure, and this storefront's pressure points arrive predictably - gameday, a roster announcement, a postseason run. A page that loads fine under normal browsing can slow to a crawl the instant thousands of fans hit the same product page at once, and the same spikes that strain a site also expose it to more malicious traffic.

Key challenges included:

  • Traffic spikes concentrated in short windows around live sporting events
  • Product and checkout pages that needed to stay fast even when demand jumped many times over baseline
  • Image-heavy merchandise pages that could easily slow load times if left unoptimized
  • Payment and checkout flows that needed to stay secure across every store and the online storefront
  • Traffic surges around gameday that increase both legitimate demand and exposure to malicious traffic
  • No tolerance for downtime once a live event or product drop begins

The Approach

Performance and security work was built around the storefront's actual traffic pattern - sharp, predictable spikes - and treated as an ongoing discipline, not a one-time pass.

Performance Auditing

Measured load speed and Core Web Vitals across key pages to identify where slowdowns were most likely to hurt conversion.

Caching & Asset Delivery

Implemented caching and image optimization so heavy merchandise pages loaded quickly even under a traffic surge.

Load Testing

Simulated gameday-level traffic ahead of high-demand dates to catch bottlenecks before fans ever hit them.

Edge Security Hardening

Configured protections at the network edge to filter malicious traffic before it ever reached the storefront.

Secure Payment Handling

Reviewed checkout and payment flows against current standards to protect fan data across every store and channel.

Uptime Monitoring

Established continuous monitoring so availability issues get caught and addressed before fans notice them.

The Solution

A performance-tuned, continuously monitored storefront now holds up under gameday-level demand, staying fast and protected when visitor volume peaks.

Capability Impact
Fast Page Loads Product and checkout pages that stay responsive under peak traffic.
Optimized Asset Delivery Image-heavy merchandise pages that load quickly without sacrificing visual quality.
Verified Load Capacity Confidence the site holds up before a high-demand event ever begins.
Edge Protection Malicious traffic filtered before it reaches the storefront.
Secure Checkout Payment flows protected across every store and channel.
Continuous Uptime Monitoring Availability issues caught and addressed before fans ever notice.

The Results

With performance tuned for the storefront's actual traffic pattern and security hardened around it, store and web performance have improved, gameday traffic has been handled without the site buckling under peak demand, and conversion has trended upward across departments over the course of the partnership.

  • Fast, stable page loads during peak gameday traffic
  • Secure, protected checkout and payment flows across every channel

Business Impact

Speed matters when it's hard to maintain - the moment thousands of fans arrive at once. Tuning the storefront around its real traffic pattern kept the site fast precisely when a slow page would have cost sales, and treating security as an ongoing discipline kept it protected and available through live events and product drops.

Through performance auditing, caching and asset optimization, load testing, and edge security hardening, this initiative created a foundation that has supported the partnership with Hawkeye Fan Shop and the University of Iowa Athletics Department since 2016.
