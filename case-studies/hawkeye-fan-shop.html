Hawkeye Fan Shop Case Study

A Storefront Built to Handle Gameday, Not Just Survive It

A look at the measurement, product-page tracking, and site reliability work behind Hawkeye Fan Shop - the official online retailer for University of Iowa Athletics.

Project Overview

Project Ongoing eCommerce & Storefront Partnership
Organization Hawkeye Fan Shop, the official online retailer for University of Iowa Athletics
Focus Measurement Strategy, Product-Page Tracking, Site Reliability
Outcome Ten years of accurate reporting and a storefront that holds up on gameday

The Challenge

Hawkeye Fan Shop runs on a calendar it doesn't control. Traffic follows the Iowa Hawkeyes schedule rather than a steady curve, and every part of the storefront - from measurement to page speed - has to hold up when a single Saturday can outweigh a normal week.

Key challenges included:

  • Gameday traffic spikes that dwarf typical retail patterns
  • Reporting that needed to hold up across years, not just a single campaign
  • Product and catalog tracking detailed enough to inform real merchandising decisions across a 100+ item catalog
  • A decade-long relationship that meant carrying measurement forward without losing historical continuity

What Was Built

Rather than a single project, the work has been a continuous relationship - measurement rebuilt as tools changed, product tracking extended as the catalog grew, and the storefront kept steady through a decade of gamedays.

hawkeyefanshop.com

The homepage leads with gameday and promotional merchandising, backed by tracking that shows which campaigns actually drive fans through to a product.

hawkeyefanshop.com/collections/just-arrived

Faceted browsing across a catalog this size only works if filter and sort interactions are tracked well enough to show what fans are actually narrowing in on.

hawkeyefanshop.com/products/iowa-hawkeyes-2026-football-schedule-tee

Product-level tracking follows each listing from view to add-to-cart, so merchandising decisions rest on real behavior instead of guesswork.

hawkeyefanshop.com/products/iowa-vertical-arch-w-vin-logo

Some products carry their own tiered pricing, like this "Buy 2 Shirts for $30" bundle. Tracking has to capture which tier a fan picks, not just that something was added to cart.

The Results

With a decade of continuous work behind it, Hawkeye Fan Shop can trust its numbers and count on the storefront when it matters most.

  • A storefront that holds up under sharp, gameday-driven traffic
  • Reporting that stakeholders can act on without a background in analytics
  • Product and catalog tracking detailed enough to guide real merchandising calls
  • A measurement setup carried forward, not rebuilt from scratch, as tools have changed

Business Impact

A retailer tied to a live sports calendar doesn't get a slow ramp-up, and a ten-year relationship doesn't run on guesswork either. Measurement has to keep pace with the catalog, the schedule, and the platform itself, all at once - and it has to hold together across channels, not just on the storefront itself.

hawkeyefanshop.com/pages/store-locations

The online storefront is one piece of a larger footprint - physical locations across Iowa plus a concessions presence at every home game at Kinnick Stadium, all reporting into the same numbers.

hawkeyefanshop.com/pages/bogo-tees

Promotions like this BOGO tee campaign get their own tracked landing page, so a sale's actual lift can be measured against the traffic and revenue it pulls in.

hawkeyefanshop.com/products/hawkeye-fan-shop-gift-card

Gift cards are redeemable both online and in-store, so revenue from a single purchase can surface in either channel - reporting has to reconcile the two, not just track them separately.

hawkeyefanshop.com/pages/sign-up

Fan Alerts signups feed the same audience that promotions like the BOGO campaign later target, so list growth and campaign performance get measured as one connected loop.

Through structured measurement and ongoing site work, this partnership has given Hawkeye Fan Shop a foundation it's built on since 2016.
