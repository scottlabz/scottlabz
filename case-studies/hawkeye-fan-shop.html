Hawkeye Fan Shop Case Study

A Storefront Built to Handle Gameday, Not Just Survive It

A look at the measurement, product-page tracking, and site reliability work behind Hawkeye Fan Shop - the official online retailer for University of Iowa Athletics.

Project Overview

Project Ongoing eCommerce & Storefront Partnership
Organization Hawkeye Fan Shop, the official online retailer for University of Iowa Athletics
Focus Measurement Strategy, Product-Page Tracking, Site Reliability
Outcome Ten years of accurate reporting and a storefront that holds up on gameday

The Challenge

Hawkeye Fan Shop runs on a calendar it doesn't control. Traffic follows the Iowa Hawkeyes schedule rather than a steady curve, and every part of the storefront - from measurement to page speed - has to hold up when a single Saturday can outweigh a normal week.

Key challenges included:

  • Gameday traffic spikes that dwarf typical retail patterns
  • Reporting that needed to hold up across years, not just a single campaign
  • Product and catalog tracking detailed enough to inform real merchandising decisions across a catalog of thousands of products.
  • A decade-long relationship that meant carrying measurement forward without losing historical continuity

What Was Built

Rather than a single project, the work has been a continuous relationship - measurement rebuilt as tools changed, product tracking extended as the catalog grew, and the storefront kept steady through a decade of gamedays.

The Results

With a decade of continuous work behind it, Hawkeye Fan Shop can trust its numbers and count on the storefront when it matters most.
