Abracalytics Case Study

An Enterprise Site, No Smoke and Mirrors

Abracalytics is an enterprise analytics consultancy. Scott Labz built its website around how enterprise buyers judge a vendor: fast, secure, measured from the first visit, and focused on one clear pitch.

Project Overview

Project A website and analytics for a new analytics consultancy
Organization Abracalytics, an enterprise analytics consultancy
Services Hosting and domain setup, brand assets, web design and build, analytics, and enterprise repositioning
Live site abracalytics.com

The Challenge

Enterprise buyers judge a consultancy before they ever talk to it. Abracalytics needed a site that made a serious first impression: credible to large organizations, clear about what it does, and short enough to read in a minute.

It also had to keep the brand's playful side, its magic theme and mascot, without undercutting the enterprise pitch.

What Was Built

Scott Labz set up the hosting and domain, produced the brand assets, designed and built the site, added analytics, and redesigned it for enterprise buyers.
