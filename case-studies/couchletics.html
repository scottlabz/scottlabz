Couchletics Case Study

A Brand and the Store Built Around It

Scott Labz designed the Couchletics brand and built its online store from scratch: a premium sleepwear and lounge line with a collegiate, game-day look, made to order and shipped without a warehouse.

Project Overview

Project Brand identity and a custom online store
Organization Couchletics, a sleepwear and lounge brand with a collegiate, game-day look and the tagline "Athletic Dept. of Rest"
Timeline Work started October 4, 2026. Live October 15, 2026
At launch 60 pieces and 11 bundled sets, 1,020 color and size options
Services Brand identity, ecommerce strategy, custom store development, product catalog and print files, payments and checkout, order automation, sales tax research and reporting, SEO, analytics, hosting and security, testing, and launch
Live site couchletics.com

The Challenge

Couchletics needed to launch a premium brand quickly, without buying inventory or running a warehouse. The client asked for a store built on its own code, with full control, rather than a store on a hosted platform.

Hosted store platforms do the basics well. The things that made this brand different would each have needed a paid add-on or a workaround there:

  • Bundled sets, like a hoodie and sweatpants sold as one "uniform," that split into separate production orders behind the scenes
  • Seasonal pieces that appear and disappear on their own dates, with "last call" and holiday order-by messages that update daily
  • Live stock read straight from the production partner, so a size that runs out shows as sold out right away
  • A sports schedule feature, The Slate, built into the store
  • Sales tax reporting that follows the client's state rules exactly, where general tools fall short

A custom build made every one of these work the way the brand needs, without add-on apps, on code the client owns.

What Was Built

Scott Labz designed the brand and built the store from the ground up: the identity, the product line, the storefront, checkout, order automation, sales tax reporting, search and analytics, and the checks that cleared it for launch.

Brand identity

A complete logo system: a laced sofa icon, a wordmark with a football for the "O," a department crest, a varsity C, a seal and a set of stitched patches. Every product got a game-reference name, like The Letterman Hoodie, The Booster Club Crewneck and The Home Uniform, and the voice stays deadpan and athletic throughout.

The seal, the sofa icon and the wordmark carry one idea across the site, the packaging and the share images: the Athletic Department of Rest.

2

The homepage changes with the football calendar on its own: the header patch, the featured photos and the limited piece all follow the season.

The patch beside the logo is drawn in code and changes through the season on its own, from a training-camp whistle to a playoff trophy. In October it's a football pumpkin.

The product line

Production is print on demand through a production partner. Each piece is made after it's ordered, mostly in US facilities, and shipped straight to the customer, so the client holds no inventory. The whole range was set up in the partner's system through its API from a single product spec. The print and embroidery files are generated in code, and the colors are held to a restricted house palette so the line reads as one premium brand.

Checkout and payments

A hosted checkout styled to the brand takes cards, digital wallets and a pay-later option. Sales tax is added automatically. Shipping starts at $8.95 and is free on orders of $125 or more. Card details only ever reach the payment processor, and the store's connection to it is limited to exactly the permissions it needs.

The cart opens as a drawer beside the product and nudges the order toward free shipping.

1

A cart that stays on the page

Adding a piece opens the cart as a drawer, with the chosen color and size and quantity controls, without leaving the product.

2

One-tap add-ons toward free shipping

Small add-ons are suggested with one-tap Add buttons, and the cart points out which one takes the order to free shipping.

3

Free shipping, shown as a goal

Shipping is $8.95 and free at $125. The bar shows how far the order is from free shipping, and tax is added at checkout.

Order automation

When an order is paid, it goes to production automatically. Unusually large or higher-risk orders are held for a quick review first. Customers get an email for every step: the order confirmation, shipping with tracking, and a review request about 12 days after it ships. Shoppers who leave checkout, and agreed to hear from the brand, get one reminder email with a link that rebuilds their cart. Gift cards arrive by email as single-use codes, and any unspent balance is reissued as a new code.

Sales tax and compliance

The client's state sales tax rules were researched from primary sources, and a monthly report matches every order's address against the state's official rate file, which gives the client what they need to file. The store also follows the federal labeling rules for clothing (every clothing page says where the piece is made), anti-spam email law, payment-network rules, and browser privacy opt-out signals.

The footer says plainly what the brand is and isn't, and lists how shoppers can pay.

1

Clear about what the brand isn't

The footer states that Couchletics is an independent brand, not affiliated with any league, school or the makers of its blanks.

2

Payment options shown up front

Accepted payment methods, including digital wallets and a pay-later option, are listed before a shopper reaches checkout.

Search and analytics

Every page carries structured data for search engines, and the store publishes a sitemap and a shopping feed covering every color and size. Each product has its own share image. Analytics tracks sales and product views without ever receiving a customer's name, email address or order number.

Security, speed and quality

The store runs on a global edge network, with HTTPS enforced, security headers on every page and rate limits on checkout and other sensitive actions. More than 50 automated tests guard pricing, shipping, tax codes, seasons and gift cards, and a 38-point launch check verifies pages, photos, checkout and payment methods against the live site.

The Results

Couchletics launched on October 15, 2026, with the full line, checkout, order automation and tax reporting in place from day one.

  • The first real order was placed and sent to production on launch day
  • 60 pieces and 11 sets at launch, with 1,020 color and size options
  • All 112 pages checked on phone and desktop at launch, and all 38 launch checks passed
  • A page-quality audit of the live homepage at launch scored 98 for performance on desktop, 100 for accessibility and 100 for SEO

Business Impact

A new brand usually has to choose between launching fast on a hosted platform and building what makes it different. Couchletics didn't have to choose: the sets, seasonal drops, live stock, The Slate and the tax reporting all shipped in the first release, on code the client owns.

With no inventory to buy and production triggered by each paid order, the store's costs follow its sales. Through custom store development and privacy-safe measurement, Couchletics starts its first season with a foundation it can grow on.
