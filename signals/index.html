Signals

Field observations on measurement, monitoring, and the subtle behaviors that often reveal more than a dashboard ever will. These pieces examine the signals, patterns, anomalies, and gaps that appear throughout digital systems and what they can tell us when we take the time to look beyond the obvious.

From analytics implementation and data quality to user behavior, tracking failures, technical inconsistencies, and unexpected patterns, each observation focuses on the details that are easy to overlook but can have a meaningful impact on decisions.

The goal isn't simply to collect more data. It's to understand what the data is actually saying, recognize when something doesn't add up, and develop better habits for finding the evidence hidden between the numbers.
