Field Notes: Building a Bar-Chart Navigation

This page exists to preview the navigation component in context. Scroll down - the bar chart stays fixed to the top of the viewport so it's reachable from anywhere on the page, without hiding behind a menu button.

Every item's bar height increases left to right, echoing the bar chart built into the S in the logo. Whichever page is active becomes the tall, solid, marked bar - so the menu itself shows you where you are, without needing a separate breadcrumb or "you are here" label.

The clickable zone for every item is a full-height column, even for short bars like "Home" - the bar is decorative, not the target. Tab through the page with your keyboard to see the focus outline; it's deliberately thick and high-contrast rather than the thin default most browsers ship.

Try narrowing your browser window. Past roughly 736px - the same breakpoint the rest of scottlabz.com already uses - the bar chart becomes a horizontally scrollable strip instead of wrapping or collapsing into a hidden menu. Every item stays visible and reachable; nothing is ever hidden behind a hamburger icon.

If your operating system has "reduce motion" turned on, the small grow-on-hover animation is skipped automatically - the state change (color, height, label weight) still happens instantly, just without the transition.
