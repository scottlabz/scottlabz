Eligibility

Who can apply

Your shelter is eligible if it:

  • Is a registered 501(c)(3) in the United States, with an EIN you can share
  • Has rescuing and rehoming dogs as a central part of its mission, with a building or a foster network
  • Has been operating for at least 6 months
  • Has no website, or one that no longer works for you
  • Can provide photos, adoption details, and contact information within 3 weeks of being selected
  • Is not a current or past paid client of Scott Labz

Selection

How shelters are chosen

Every application is read and scored on four things:

  • Impact How much a new site would change how adopters, fosters, and donors find and reach you.
  • A clear goal More adoption inquiries, more foster sign-ups, more donations. Something specific to measure.
  • Readiness Time and willingness to share content, answer questions, and review drafts.
  • Community What your shelter means to the dogs and the people around it.

The process

How it works

Applications are read as they come in. A limited number of shelters are selected at a time, so each site gets full attention.

Questions

What shelters usually ask

Is there really no cost?

The design, build, and launch are free. You pay for your own domain and hosting, which stay in your name. No purchase, payment, or social media post is required to apply.

How do you confirm our 501(c)(3) status?

With the EIN on your application, checked against the IRS's public list of tax-exempt organizations. Only organizations with current 501(c)(3) status can be selected.

Do we need a building to apply?

No. Foster-based shelters and rescues can apply, as long as the organization is a registered 501(c)(3).

Does it matter where we're located?

No. Any registered 501(c)(3) dog shelter in the United States can apply. The work is done remotely.

We take in other animals too. Can we still apply?

Yes, as long as rescuing and rehoming dogs is a central part of your mission.

Who owns the finished website?

You do. The site, its content, and the domain belong to your shelter.

How do we keep our adoptable dogs current?

The approach gets settled before the build starts, based on how your staff and volunteers work. If you already keep listings on an adoption service, the page can point to them.

Can donors give through the site?

Your site includes a clear Donate button that goes to the giving page you already use. Taking payments on the site itself isn't included.

What if we already have a website?

You can still apply. Share what isn't working about it, whether that's how it looks, how it performs, or that people can't find what they need.

Is this a raffle or drawing?

No. Every application is read and scored against the criteria on this page. Shelters are selected by review, not by chance.

What happens if we're not selected?

A limited number of shelters are selected at a time, and only selected shelters are contacted directly. If you haven't heard back, you're welcome to apply again.

The fine print

Program rules

  1. No purchase necessary. No purchase, payment, follow, share, or review is required to apply or to be selected.
  2. Who can apply. Dog shelters and rescues in the United States with current 501(c)(3) status, operating for at least 6 months, where rescuing and rehoming dogs is a central part of the mission. The applicant must be a director, officer, or manager of the organization, 18 or older. Current and past paid clients of Scott Labz are not eligible.
  3. Applications. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis, one per organization at a time. Scott Labz may pause or close applications at any time.
  4. How shelters are chosen. Every eligible application is read and scored on impact, a clear goal, readiness, and community. Finalists are contacted for a short call. A limited number of shelters are selected at a time, by review, not by chance. Selected shelters are contacted directly, and other applicants are not contacted individually.
  5. What's covered. The work listed under "What every selected shelter receives." Domain and hosting fees remain the shelter's responsibility. The offer has no cash value and cannot be transferred.
  6. If a shelter can't proceed. If a selected shelter cannot provide photos, adoption details, and contact information within 3 weeks of being selected, the spot may go to another finalist.
  7. After launch. Selected shelters agree to a published case study, a small footer credit, and check-ins at 30, 60, and 90 days, all prepared and handled by Scott Labz. A testimonial is welcome but not required.
  8. Your information. Application details are used only to review your application and contact you about it, and are never sold. The full Privacy Policy is at scottlabz.com/trust/privacy.
