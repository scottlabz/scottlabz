Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you replace our existing advertising setup?

No. The focus is improving the practice already in place - connecting existing campaigns to clean measurement and a repeatable management process. If a new approach is needed, it can be implemented.

Do you handle both strategy and day-to-day management?

Yes. Campaign strategy, media buying, and ongoing management are handled together, so planning and execution stay connected instead of splitting across separate hand-offs.

How do you know a campaign is actually working?

Conversion tracking and attribution are built to tie spend directly to outcomes, replacing platform-reported estimates with a clearer picture of what's actually driving results - see how that played out in the Advertising & Media Buying case study.
