1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how Scott Labz LLC ("Company," "we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies to recognize you when you visit our website at https://scottlabz.com ("Website").

It explains what these technologies are, why we use them, and your rights to control our use of them.

In some cases, we may use cookies to collect personal information or information that becomes personal information when combined with other data.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. Website owners commonly use cookies to make websites work, operate more efficiently, and provide reporting information.

  • First-party cookies are set by the website owner, in this case, Scott Labz.
  • Third-party cookies are set by parties other than the website owner. They enable third-party features or functionality, such as advertising, interactive content, and analytics.

Third parties that set cookies may recognize your device when it visits our Website and certain other websites.

3. Why Do We Use Cookies?

We use cookies only to understand how our Website is used. The Website doesn't need cookies to work: it has no accounts or logins, and it sets no strictly necessary cookies.

Our analytics cookies help us see which pages visitors use and how they found us, so we can measure our marketing and improve the Website.

4. How Can I Control Cookies?

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (see section 6), or block Google Analytics with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

If you reject cookies, you can still use every part of our Website.

5. Do My Cookie Choices Apply to All My Devices?

No. Your cookie choices are saved in the browser you used to make them and apply only to that browser on that device.

You'll need to make your choices again when you:

  • Visit using a different device or browser.
  • Use a private or incognito window.
  • Clear your cookies or browser data.

6. How Can I Control Cookies in My Browser?

The steps for managing cookies vary by browser. Visit your browser's help menu for more information.

7. What About Other Tracking Technologies?

Cookies are not the only technologies used to recognize or track website visitors. Others include web beacons, tracking pixels, and clear GIFs: small graphics files containing unique identifiers.

Apart from Google Analytics, our Website doesn't use web beacons, tracking pixels, or clear GIFs.

8. Do We Serve Targeted Advertising?

No. We don't show ads on our Website, and our Website doesn't set advertising cookies.
